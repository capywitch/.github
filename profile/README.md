## About TCGRP

TCGRP (TCG Rio Preto & Região) is an independent technology initiative focused on the Trading Card Game community.

Our goal is to connect players, local game stores, tournament organizers, and content creators through digital tools that make the TCG ecosystem more accessible, organized, and collaborative.

What started as a regional platform has grown into an ecosystem of web applications designed to support different aspects of the hobby.

We believe technology can help communities grow by making information accessible and providing practical tools for players and organizers.

## Our Projects

The TCGRP ecosystem currently includes three web applications.

### 1. TCGRP — Community Platform

🌐 **[tcgrp.com.br](https://tcgrp.com.br)**

The central hub for the TCG community in São José do Rio Preto and surrounding cities in Brazil.

The platform connects players and stores, bringing tournament information and community content together in one place.

**Features**
- Tournament calendar and event discovery.
- Store directory and local community information.
- Dedicated pages for different Trading Card Games.
- Articles, guides, and community content.
- Player profiles and participation tools.
- Administrative tools for partner stores.
- Tournament management and recurring events.

**Supported games include:**

Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, Digimon, One Piece, Gundam, Flesh and Blood, Riftbound, Sorcery, Yu-Gi-Oh! and others.

### 2. TCGRP Oracle

🔮 **[oracle.tcgrp.com.br](https://oracle.tcgrp.com.br)**

A dedicated application in the TCGRP ecosystem for card information and reference.

The Oracle project provides a separate environment for card-related functionality, allowing it to evolve independently from the main community platform.

### 3. MTG Bracket Analyzer

🃏 **[bracket.tcgrp.com.br](https://bracket.tcgrp.com.br)**

An open-source-oriented tool for analyzing Magic: The Gathering decks and their Commander brackets.

The project aims to help players understand deck composition and evaluate how their decks fit into the bracket system.

Its development focuses on a modular analysis engine and a web interface, with an architecture that supports independent evolution of the analysis logic and user experience.

## Our Philosophy

- **Community first:** Technology should help players, stores, and organizers.
- **Accessibility:** Make useful information and tools easy to find and use.
- **Transparency:** Build understandable systems and document important decisions.
- **Open development:** Encourage collaboration, experimentation, and knowledge sharing.
- **Continuous improvement:** Evolve our tools based on real community needs.
